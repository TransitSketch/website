TransitSketch

Sketch a transit network. See who it serves.

TransitSketch is a free, open-source tool for researchers, advocates and planners. Draw routes and timetables on a map, then measure access to jobs, schools, groceries and more, and how fairly that access is shared.

Sign up for the beta

Sketch first

Draw routes, stops and service on a map, or start from an existing GTFS feed. Compare scenarios side by side. It feels like a planning tool, not a data-entry form.

Measure access

Count the opportunities each neighbourhood can reach by transit, using real timetables and street networks, for any day and time of day.

Ask who benefits

Bring in census data and see how access is shared across groups: averages, inequality ratios and transport disadvantage, with charts and maps ready to export.

Privacy

We collect the details you give in the form (your name, email, role, organisation and what you'd use TransitSketch for) only to contact you about beta testing. We don't share them with anyone or add you to any other mailing list. The form is run by Tally, which stores the answers on our behalf. We delete the list once beta testing ends.
